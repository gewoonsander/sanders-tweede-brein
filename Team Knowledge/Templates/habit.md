# Morning Walk

## Why this habit

The reason this habit earns a place in your life. What you are hoping it does for you. What changes if you keep it for a year.

## What it looks like

The concrete shape of the habit. Time of day, location, duration, what counts as "done." If you cannot describe the habit in one sentence, it is too vague to stick.

## Reflection

Daily check-ins use this shape so the derived Tracking dashboard can mirror them:

```markdown
### YYYY-MM-DD

- done: true
- amount: 25
- unit: herhalingen
- trigger: close-session
- note: optional context
```

Omit `amount`, `unit`, and `note` when they do not apply.
