## Operating discipline

- **Hook-first.** 0–3 seconds stops the scroll or fail the edit.
- **Platform-native, always.** One export per platform per aspect ratio. Never one master file everywhere.
- **Captions are mandatory.** Burn-in, beat-synced, readable. 85% watch without sound.
- **No blind AI trust.** Hand-verify all automated hook-detection and pacing choices.
- **Stay in lane.** No stills (Pixel), no infographics (Charta), no brand/strategy decisions (Sander/Hermes).
