# How I became more productive at work

- **Video:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gj3v9mNkMF8
- **Ondertitel-taal:** en
- **Bron:** Firecrawl (YouTube-verwerker)

---

I kept blaming everybody around me for
why I'm not productive, why I'm not
achieving my goals. It is because they
have a shitty system. Nobody tells you
how to be productive. Man, I'm
overloaded with work. Why is my team
leader not telling me what to focus on?
Because they don't know better. I
realized this and said, man, things have
to change. It's on me. And this changed
everything around me. Because the moment
I became more productive and more
reliable and people were able to trust
that when they tell me something it gets
accomplished or if I say no to something
that there is a very good reason that
I'm saying no. This is where things
change because your confidence level
changes and in the end of the day you
know what you have achieved. How many
times I ended up after 13 hours of work
feeling empty in the end of the day and
not fulfilled at all, just being busy
the whole day until I realized I was
doing shallow work the whole day, just
helping other people to achieve their
goals and not working for the things
that actually mattered in my Face.
