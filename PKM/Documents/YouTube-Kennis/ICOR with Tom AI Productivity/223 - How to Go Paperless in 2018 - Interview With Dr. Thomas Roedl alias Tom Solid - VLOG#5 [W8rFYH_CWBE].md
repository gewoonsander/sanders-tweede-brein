Bernd Martin: Welcome, Tom Solid.
Today, we are in a café in Kempten (Germany)
Tom, you are from Allgäu (Bavaria) and now you are living in the Black Forest.
Do you miss your home country?
Tom: I do miss it very much!
And I tell you why. In the black forest you also have Mountains, trees
and everything you have in Bavaria too, but there are just to less lakes.
Bernd: Ok, keyword "trees".
"Go Paperless" Tom: Hug a tree. Bernd: Hug a tree.
Tom: This is my mantra, exactly.
Of course, it is easy to say
if you go paperless, you will save paper,
and hence, less trees will die.
Bernd: Tom Solid owns a very popular Youtube Channel
which grew exponentially
You started your channel about half a year ago. How many subscribers do you have right now?
Tom: The channel is close to reach 4000 subscribers.
Bernd: 4000 in such a short time.
It really is a topic that has increasing demand and just fits in our times.
Tom: Go Paperless is your topic,
how do you define this in simple words?
Go Paperless is like
the name already states it. Stop using paper.
So the general "abstinence" of paper.
The freedom to live without paper. Bernd: Ok,
would you say you are more a greenie or a techie?
Tom: I would say I am more the techie.
About a year ago I thought I would never go paperless,
because in my eyes technology was not ready back then.
Couldn´t imagine to a pencil like the Apple Pencil
which is performing good enough to keep up with my writing.
It never felt natural enough, which is why I always came back to pen and paper.
This changed with the Apple Pencil.
I am a convinced fan boy.
hashtag not sponsored
Bernd: If somebody comes to you and says, "hey I like the topic
Go Paperless, I want to go paperless too.
what do I need to go paperless?"
as a private person.
Tom: Exactly, this would be my first question
is it a company or is a private person, which are completely different starting points.
If you are a private person
it is important to know, in which area you want to go paperless.
Do you just want to write a daily journal,
Or do you want to digitalize your contracts?
Depending on this question, there a several different systems you would use for each case.
One thing to keep in mind is, that many tools save data in the cloud.
And especially with your sensitive data, this needs to be chosen very carfully.
Bernd: So I need a scanner to digitalize the documents
that I still get via normal mail, contracts, bills etc.
And another thing is then
a tablet you can write on.
Of course there are scanners, also cheap ones to digitalize your documents.
But there are also apps, that do the job very well and which I use personally too.
As tablet I use the iPad Pro.
I know that Surface from Microsoft also exists, which seems to be a good alternative, but I don´t have any experiences with it yet.
But in general, there are enough ways to have a paper based notebook in a digital form.
To write a daily journal.
Bernd: Sounds very interesting.
If you want to learn more, I highly recommend to have a look at
Tom Solids channel. He is doing a great job.
He puts a lot of effort into it.
You have more to offer besides the Youtube channel, right?
Tom: I have more, yes. We are a very fast growing paperless community.
It is called #PaperlessMe. I also founded a company with this name.
So there is the website (www.tomsolid.com) where I write my blogs.
There is also a forum for discussions about note taking apps.
Then there is a Facebook group called PaperlessMe
This group is growing very fast. And it is nice to see
how people engage in there.
It is an international channel/group, which means we only talk/write in english.
Bernd: Yes, I have to add, Tom Solids channel is in english
which enables him to reach a much broader audience.
And he is watched worldwide.
Tom: Excactly. There is a german speaking group called Paperless Pioneers. Which is worth to check out
if you are a german speaker.
Bernd: Thank you very much Tom Solid for this quick interview.
We will follow this topic definitely.
